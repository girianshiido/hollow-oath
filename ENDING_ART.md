# Ending illustrations

Four original ending key illustrations were generated with the built-in image generation tool, using each existing class portrait as an identity reference. The final optimized assets are:

- `public/art/ending-vanguard.webp`
- `public/art/ending-hexweaver.webp`
- `public/art/ending-nightblade.webp`
- `public/art/ending-oathkeeper.webp`

All four are 1024 × 1536. The End and the character name are rendered by the game, so names stay editable and text stays sharp.

## Prompt set

Shared prompt: “Create a new finished ending key illustration for the English dark fantasy manga game Hollow Oath. Preserve the reference hero’s recognizable face, gender, hair, clothing and equipment identity. Depict the same hero victorious and contemplative at dawn overlooking a rescued medieval kingdom, with a broken dark eclipse dispersing into warm clouds. Ultra detailed serious hand painted manga, elegant cinematic lighting and intricate material textures matching the reference. Vertical portrait composition, heroic three-quarter or full length figure, face in upper third, scenic background to image edges. Center lower half needs darker quiet visual space for an HTML title overlay. Beautiful hopeful mood after long hardship. No text, typography, logo or watermark.”

Class variations:

- Vanguard: sword planted by a ruined battlement, red cloak in sunrise.
- Hexweaver: violet magical embers fade above her open grimoire on a tower overlooking dawn.
- Nightblade: lowered twin blades on a forest ridge above the city, green cloak.
- Oathkeeper: shield at rest amid white stone sanctuary ruins, teal dawn and gold light.
