# Crypt Pikeman — three-pose rehearsal

An isolated visual prototype, accessible at `?sprite-test=pikeman` on the published game. The main campaign continues using its original single sprites. This rehearsal creates only disposable in-memory runs and does not call save or achievement APIs. It uses the real combat engine and real spell effects, with 32 identical Crypt Pikemen, fixed statistics and fresh HP/MP between opponents.

## Final assets and framing

- Resting pose: `public/art/foe-2.webp` (original, unchanged).
- Attack contact: `public/art/foe-2-attack-v2.webp`.
- Receiving a hit: `public/art/foe-2-hurt.webp`.

The two new poses were made with the built-in image generation tool, editing the original sprite. All three delivery frames share the same 305 × 358 canvas and transparency. Mechanical PNG-to-WebP conversion/downsampling matches the existing mobile sprite. A fixed-aspect render box keeps the same scale on all three frames. Small translations align the foot baseline: attack (+6,+8) and hit (−3,+3) in source-canvas pixels, scaled with the render box. No per-pose zoom is used. The first generated attack was rejected because its weapon was still raised; the downward revision places the axe head low at the end of its arc. The selected v2 swings the entire weapon toward the lower right, with the shaft running from upper left to lower right. The arms follow the descending strike while the lower-body placement and scale are retained. An intermediate leftward head-roll correction was discarded. The standing and approved hit images are unchanged.

Attack is displayed for 240 ms and hit reaction for 190 ms. Closely timed strikes are queued in order, with a short bounded queue cleared between opponents. Options offer original-image comparison, pause, half speed, still-frame inspection and effect visibility. The rehearsal is deliberately not rolled out to ordinary campaign encounters before user review.
