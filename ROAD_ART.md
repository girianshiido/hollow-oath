# Roadside encounter illustrations

Six original illustrations generated using the built-in image generation tool. The game selects twelve of eighteen authored encounters per adventure; scenes share these six setting illustrations. Final files are listed below and are included in the offline and portable editions.

## Shared art direction

Use case: illustration-story. Original in-game narrative illustration for Hollow Oath. Serious, extremely polished manga-inspired dark fantasy painted concept art, realistically detailed gothic ruins, cinematic light, charcoal stone, muted teal shadows and antique gold, restrained emotion. ## Scene prompts and final files

### public/art/road-shrine.webp

A cracked roadside shrine beneath a leafless silver tree, small candles and a shallow bowl reflecting an eclipsed moon. A narrow worn pilgrim trail, carved protective hands in the stone, an inviting yet uncanny sanctuary.

### public/art/road-prisoner.webp

An exhausted but dignified hooded traveler in weathered medieval clothes sits beside an old cage on a drowned causeway. One hand reaches through a loosened chain, small warm lantern hanging nearby. A human face, compassionate, no wounds or violence.

### public/art/road-ferry.webp

A lone hooded ferryman stands in a long narrow boat at a ruined stone landing. Black water mirrors golden lamps and an eclipsed moon. A coiled rope and sealed letter on the landing, a distant city drowned in mist.

### public/art/road-forge.webp

An old human armorer sits at a tiny glowing forge beneath a collapsed gothic arch. Tools, a chipped sword, a ceremonial shield and a bundle of letters. The forge is a haven of amber warmth amid the cold ruined kingdom.

### public/art/road-mirror.webp

An abandoned moonlit observatory alcove with a tall cracked black mirror, fine silver constellation lines visible within its glass. A broken astrolabe and folded map sit on its stone desk, luminous threads lead into the mirror.

### public/art/road-garden.webp

A secret garden in the ruins of a royal cloister, pale luminous flowers growing through broken paving. A weathered human stone statue offers an open hand, small birds and long golden ribbons catch the wind. Gentle hope beneath the eclipse.
