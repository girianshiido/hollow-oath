# Illustrated Chronicle

Twelve original illustrations generated with the built-in image generation tool for Hollow Oath. Final game assets are `public/art/journal-<wave>-<variant>.webp`; the build includes them in the offline cache and portable release.

Art direction: serious, richly painted manga dark fantasy consistent with Hollow Oath; cinematic environments, weathered gothic stone and metal, restrained spectral light, no typography or watermark. These are narrative illustrations, not combat sprites. Each scene is presented in a 3:2 frame in the journal and can be enlarged without cropping.

## Scene prompt set and saved assets

### public/art/journal-4-a.webp

A drowned stone causeway at low tide, a broken monument bearing thirty-two worn guardian reliefs. Names on the monument have been deliberately scraped away. Vast bleak estuary, chains disappearing underwater, cold silver dawn beneath an eclipse. No legible writing.

### public/art/journal-8-a.webp

Inside an ancient lunar observatory: an enormous crescent-shaped brass engine suspends a dark artificial moon, thirty-two thin golden conduits converge on it. A small human silhouette below conveys immense scale. Violet and tarnished gold light.

### public/art/journal-8-b.webp

The same ancient lunar engine seen from a high shattered balcony, concentric astrolabe rings cast crescent shadows on the floor, golden conduits connect a captive black moon. Dust illuminated by violet beams. Different viewpoint, same revelation.

### public/art/journal-12-a.webp

A submerged gothic belfry revealed below still black water, giant bronze bell inside, ghostly silver human silhouettes rise like echoes around it. A faint moonlit shore above, solemn and mournful, no text.

### public/art/journal-16-a.webp

A magnificent gilded prison chapel, a sealed iron sarcophagus standing upright in the center beneath a thin shaft of pale green light, tarnished angelic armor and chains, one gauntlet extending in a protective gesture. Solemn, not horror gore.

### public/art/journal-16-b.webp

Close view within a ruined gilded prison: the iron sarcophagus seen from its side, a broken gold seal reveals a gentle luminous human hand inside, carved oath motifs and heavy chains. Dark teal and warm gold, emotionally humane.

### public/art/journal-20-a.webp

A memory of a noble medieval king before the catastrophe, kneeling to accept a heavy dark crown while holding the hand of a frightened child behind him, distant city threatened by a cosmic storm. The king looks compassionate and exhausted, warm memory light against incoming darkness.

### public/art/journal-24-a.webp

An immense wheel of penitence in a ruined sanctuary, concentric rings of anonymous kneeling stone generations, a thin stream of golden light flowing from many small votive flames into the wheel. The eclipse visible through a broken roof. Ritual continuing across centuries.

### public/art/journal-24-b.webp

The wheel of penitence seen through an abandoned processional corridor; successive rows of worn statues each carry the same burden, their golden threads feeding a vast turning ring in the distance. An extinguished brazier foreground, cool gray and dying amber.

### public/art/journal-28-a.webp

Inside a puppet reliquary, a hollow royal throne suspended by fine luminous threads attached to an empty crown, shadow of an imprisoned exhausted king behind gauze, the crown is visibly the controller. Intricate gothic theatrical machinery, crimson and spectral gold.

### public/art/journal-28-b.webp

Close symbolic view: an empty black crown floats above a royal hand whose fingers are bound by glowing puppet strings; behind, the same immense reliquary throne and a human silhouette trapped in translucent amber. Clear tragic captivity, crimson and cold blue.

### public/art/journal-32-a.webp

The shattered black crown lies on the steps of the Hollow Throne. Its thirty-two golden links dissolve into ascending lights, pale sunrise enters the ruined royal hall for the first time, distant kingdom roofs beyond open doors. Quiet liberation and hope, no living hero needed.

## Replay selection

Waves 8, 16, 24 and 28 each have two illustrations of the same revelation. The saved adventure identity deterministically selects each variant, so reloading, renaming or importing a save preserves its illustrations. New adventures can reveal different combinations. No additional player choice or gameplay reward is tied to the image variant.
