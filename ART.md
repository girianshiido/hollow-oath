# Art production

The illustrations were created for this game with the built-in ImageGen tool. The contact sheets were cut into individual assets, trimmed for full-sprite framing, and encoded as WebP. Portraits, environments, and sprites are in `public/art/`. In-game color variants use the original silhouettes with changes to their palette. Combat effects and the interface icons are drawn by the game.
